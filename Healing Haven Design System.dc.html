07 · Motion

Everything moves like an exhale

One principle governs all of it: slow, eased, and settled — never snappy, never bouncing. Motion arrives, holds long enough to be read, and leaves gently. It runs at two tempos, and every pattern below resolves to a clean, readable still for anyone who opts out of motion.

Tempo A · Interface

0.2 – 0.4s

Hovers, focus, card lifts, menu reveals. Fades and 1–2px lifts, never bounce.

cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)
Tempo B · Brand & video

2 – 4s reveals

Reels, Stories, the logo sting. Elements settle in, hold, then ease away. Long holds are the whole point.

Rules that keep motion on-brand
  • Never bounce, spring, or overshoot — softness comes from long duration, not elastic easing.
  • Always hold. Anything that appears stays on screen long enough to be read before it leaves.
  • One thing moves at a time, or things move in a gentle stagger — never a busy simultaneous entrance.
  • Every pattern has a reduced-motion still: the final, legible frame. Motion is enhancement, never the only way to read it.
