Reassurance, fine print, and "what to expect" notes beside the main flow.

```jsx
<AsideCard label="Reassurance" title="The hardest step" accent="fire">
  Reaching out is the hardest step. There's no obligation after it.
</AsideCard>
```

`fire` accent for the emotional reassurance, `gold` for practical notes, `wood` for logistics. One per screen.
