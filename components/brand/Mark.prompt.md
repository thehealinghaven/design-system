The brand mark — use it wherever the practice signs an asset (header, letterhead, slide corner, social card).

```jsx
<Mark size={52} color="var(--gold-light)" />
```

Only two colors: `var(--gold)` (or `--gold-deep`) on cream, `var(--gold-light)` on charcoal/wood-deep. Never recolor to fire, seasonal, or gradient. Never place gold on pure white. Pillar accent color lives in the kicker pill and footer rule, never on the mark.
