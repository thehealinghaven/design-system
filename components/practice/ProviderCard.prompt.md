One provider, in the directory or on their own bio page.

```jsx
<ProviderCard
  name="Helen De-Lovely" credential="LCSW" role="Founder & Clinical Director"
  location="Salt Lake City · In-person & Online" photo="/assets/imagery/hero-shot.jpg"
  element="fire" acceptingNew
  specialties={['Somatic work', 'EMDR', 'Adult ADHD']}
  action={<Button variant="underline">Learn how I work</Button>}
  interactive>
  Therapy here is slow. That's the point.
</ProviderCard>
```

**The element accent.** Every provider is assigned one of the five elements once, at
onboarding, and keeps it across their card, bio page, tags and social captions. It's
the only thing that makes their surfaces theirs. Two house rules bend for this and
only this: a provider directory may show more than two element colors in one layout,
and gold does not have to lead in a card whose provider is wood or water. Fire is
reserved for Helen — it's her signature.

**Bio copy is provider voice** — first person, their own sign-off. The rest of the
directory page (eyebrow, intro, CTA) is brand voice. See the *Voice · two tiers*
guideline.

**Don't:** no credential inflation (write the license, not the aspiration), no
supervised associate shown without their supervising clinician named nearby, no
outcome claims in the bio, no stock headshot, no square-cropped photo — the petal
frame is what makes a face read as this practice.
