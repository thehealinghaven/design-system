Voice · two tiers

Who is speaking?

The original guide assumed one clinician, so every published line said I. With more than one provider and more than one city, I has to belong to a person and the brand needs a voice of its own. Two tiers, one rule: the brand never says I, and a provider never speaks for the brand.

Tier 1

Brand voice

The Healing Haven · third person · no sign-off

Person
Third person. "The Healing Haven", "the practice", "your provider". "You" for the reader.
We
Only for the collective of providers — "the providers here", not "we'll talk for fifteen minutes".
Sign-off
None. The mark signs it.
Job
Explain what exists, who it's for, what it costs, how to start.

"Every provider here owns their practice. What they share is a standard of care."

"I'll help you find the right fit." — the brand has no I.

Tier 2

Provider voice

A named clinician · first person · signs off

Person
First person singular. "I", their own name.
We
Unchanged from the original guide — the shared work of a session. "We move at the speed of safety."
Sign-off
Em dash + first name, e.g. "— Helen". Never "— The Healing Haven".
Job
Say how they work and what it's like to sit with them.

"Therapy here is slow. That's the point. — Helen"

"We now offer GLP-1 management at all locations." — that's brand voice.
